# Detailed Price Analysis: Tanzanian Gift/Delivery Service

## Comprehensive Pricing Structure Based on Market Research (November 2024)

### **PRICING TIER STRATEGY**

Based on competitive analysis of Giiftee, Flowers4Tanzania, StayRunners, and market gap analysis, we recommend a 3-tier pricing structure:

| **Tier** | **Price Range (TZS)** | **Price Range (USD)** | **Target Market** | **Order Volume** |
|----------|----------------------|----------------------|-------------------|------------------|
| **Entry Level** | 25,000 - 40,000 TZS | $10 - $16 | Budget-conscious, first-time buyers | 60% of orders |
| **Mid-Range** | 50,000 - 85,000 TZS | $20 - $34 | Gift-givers for special occasions | 30% of orders |
| **Premium/Luxury** | 100,000 - 300,000+ TZS | $40 - $120+ | Corporate, major milestones, "big gestures" | 10% of orders |

## H. PRICING STRATEGY RECOMMENDATIONS

### **A. Entry Point Pricing**
- **Launch with**: 25,000-35,000 TZS entry tier (bouquet + delivery)
- **Capture**: 60% of market who are price-sensitive first-time buyers
- **Upsell pathway**: Clear progression to mid-tier (50,000-80,000 TZS) and premium (100,000+ TZS)

### **B. Tier Clear Labeling**
Display all 3 tiers prominently at checkout (like Purpink does):
1. **Budget** (25,000-40,000 TZS) - "Everyday gifting"
2. **Standard** (50,000-85,000 TZS) - "Special occasions"
3. **Premium** (100,000-300,000+ TZS) - "Big gestures & corporate"

### **C. Psychological Pricing Tactics**
- **End in 5,000 or 9,000** (e.g., 45,000 TZS vs. 44,900 TZS)
- **Show "from"** prices (e.g., "Flowers from 25,000 TZS")
- **Bundle savings** displayed (e.g., "Save 5,000 TZS when you add chocolate")

### **D. Seasonal Pricing Adjustments**
- **Valentine's/Eid/Christmas**: +10-15% across all tiers (with clear communication)
- **Regular periods**: Base pricing only
- **Communicate surcharges upfront** at search/checkout
