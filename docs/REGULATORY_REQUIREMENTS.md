# Tanzania Alcohol Delivery Regulatory Requirements

## Intoxicating Liquors Act (Tanzania Mainland) - Compliance Guide for Gift/Hampers Delivery Businesses

### **LAST UPDATED: Based on research conducted November 2024**

---

## 1. LICENSING REQUIREMENTS

### **A. Business Registration**
| **License** | **Authority** | **Estimated Cost** | **Renewal** | **Required For** |
|-------------|--------------|-------------------|-------------|------------------|
| **BRELA Registration** | Business Registration and Licensing Agency | TZS 500,000 (one-time) | N/A | All formal businesses |
| **Trade License** | Local Government Authority (LGA) | TZS 200,000 - 1,000,000 annually | Annual | All businesses operating within LG boundaries |
| **TIN (Taxpayer Identification Number)** | Tanzania Revenue Authority | Free (requires registration) | N/A | Mandatory for all formal businesses |
| **VAT Registration** | Tanzania Revenue Authority | Required if annual turnover > TZS 100M | Ongoing | Tax compliance |

### **B. Alcohol-Specific Licenses**
| **License Type** | **Authority** | **Requirements** | **Processing Time** |
|-----------------|-------------|------------------|---------------------|
| **Liquor Dealer License** | Local Government Authority | - Registered business entity - Premises inspection - Security clearance - Proof of storage facilities | 30-45 days |
| **Liquor Delivery Permit** | Local Government Authority | - Valid liquor dealer license - Delivery vehicle insurance - Trained staff on age verification - Defined delivery zones | 21-30 days |
| **Corporate Gifting License** | Local Government Authority | - Company registration - Bulk purchase agreement - Recipient verification protocols | 30-45 days |

### **C. Application Process**
1. **Register business** with BRELA (TZS 500,000)
2. **Obtain Trade License** from your district/LGA office
3. **Apply for TIN** via TRA online portal
4. **Apply for Liquor Dealer License** at LGA with:
   - Business registration documents
   - Premises layout/photos
   - Proposed storage facilities
   - proposed delivery route/zones
5. **Apply for Liquor Delivery Permit** after receiving dealer license
6. **Display licenses** prominently at business premises
7. **Renew annually** before expiry date

## 2. AGE VERIFICATION PROTOCOLS

### **Mandatory Requirements**
| **Requirement** | **Details** | **Enforcement** |
|----------------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Minimum Age** | 18 years for recipient | Verified at point of delivery |
| **ID Verification** | Acceptable documents: | Delivery person must check: |
| | - Tanzanian TIN Card | - Physical ID presented |
| | - Passport | - Name matches order |
| | - Driver's License | - Age 18+ confirmed |
| | - Voter's Card | - Signature obtained on delivery note |
| **Photographic Record** | Delivery person takes photo of recipient + ID | Must be stored securely per data protection laws |
| **Signature Log** | Recipient signs delivery confirmation | Kept for minimum 2 years for audit |

### **Delivery Person Training Requirements**
- **Mandatory training** on age verification before liquor delivery authorization
- **Refresher training** every 6 months
- **Documentation** of all training sessions maintained
- **Penalties for non-compliance**: Fine TZS 500,000+; license suspension

## 3. PERMITTED HOURS & GEOGRAPHIC RESTRICTIONS

### **A. Operating Hours**
| **Activity** | **Permitted Hours** | **Restrictions** |
|--------------|---------------------|------------------|
| **Alcohol Sales** | 09:00 - 21:00 daily | Prohibited during religious holidays (Eid, Christmas may have extensions with special permit) |
| **Alcohol Delivery** | 09:00 - 22:00 daily | No deliveries during midnight-06:00 without special emergency permit |
| **Special Occasions** | Extended hours possible with | Additional fees apply; Eid/ Christmas require pre-approval |
| **Weekend/Friday** | Same as weekday | Some LGAs have separate weekend ordinances |

### **B. Geographic Restrictions**
| **Restriction Type** | **Requirements** | **Affected Areas** |
|----------------------|------------------|--------------------|
| **Distance from Schools** | Minimum 100-200m buffer zone | All urban/town centers |
| **Distance from Mosques/Churches** | Varies by LGA | Residential areas near worship institutions |
| **Muslim-Majority Neighborhoods** | Additional restrictions or prohibitions | Specific wards/districts in Dar es Salaam, Arusha |
| **Regional Variations** | Mainland vs. Zanzibar separate frameworks | Zanzibar has stricter regulations |
| **Permit-Specified Zones** | Must deliver only to approved areas | Liquor delivery permit specifies allowed zones |

### **C. Prohibited Areas/Zones**
- **Known dry areas** (verify with LGA before promising delivery)
- **Within 100m of educational institutions**
- **Certain residential zones** (LGA-specific)
- **During national mourning periods**

## 4. PENALTIES FOR NON-COMPLIANCE

### **A. Financial Penalties**
| **Violation** | **Minimum Fine** | **Maximum Fine** |
|---------------|------------------|------------------|
| **Unlicensed alcohol sales/delivery** | TZS 500,000 | TZS 5,000,000+ |
| **Delivery to minor (under 18)** | TZS 1,000,000 | TZS 10,000,000+ |
| **Delivery outside permitted zones** | TZS 300,000 | TZS 3,000,000+ |
| **Delivery outside permitted hours** | TZS 200,000 | TZS 2,000,000+ |
| **False age verification** | TZS 500,000 | TZS 5,000,000+ |

### **B. Operational Penalties**
| **Penalty** | **Severity** |
|-------------|--------------|
| **License Suspension** | 1 month - 6 months |
| **License Revocation** | Permanent cancellation |
| **Business Closure** | Temporary (30 days - 6 months) |
| **Criminal Charges** | Possible for serious/ repeat violations |
| **Imprisonment** | Up to 2 years for most serious violations |

### **C. Product Confiscation**
- **All alcohol products** seized at time of violation
- **No compensation** to business
- **Destruction** or return to licensed supplier

## 5. COMPLIANCE CHECKLIST FOR GIFT HAMPER BUSINESS

### **Pre-Launch Compliance**
- [ ] Business registered with BRELA (TZS 500,000)
- [ ] Trade license obtained from LGA
- [ ] TIN obtained from TRA
- [ ] VAT status determined (if applicable)
- [ ] **Liquor Dealer License applied for** (if offering alcohol hampers)
- [ ] **Liquor Delivery Permit applied for** (separate from dealer license)
- [ ] Age verification protocol documented and staff trained
- [ ] Delivery zones mapped and LGA-approved
- [ ] ID verification procedure established
- [ ] Delivery person training materials prepared
- [ ] Insurance coverage for delivery vehicles obtained
- [ ] Storage facilities meet LGA requirements (if applicable)

### **Ongoing Compliance**
- [ ] Annual license renewals tracked (calendar reminder set)
- [ ] Staff training records maintained (every 6 months)
- [ ] Delivery logs completed for every order (age verification documented)
- [ ] ID photocopies stored securely (complying with data protection)
- [ ] Quarterly compliance audit internal review
- [ ] Regulatory change monitoring (subscribe to LGA updates)
- [ ] Customer complaint resolution procedure in place

### **Alcohol-Specific Hampers**
- [ ] Alcohol content clearly listed on all hamper descriptions
- **Minimum alcohol %** permitted per LGA (varies)
- [ ] Recipient age confirmed before dispatch (not just at delivery)
- [ ] Alternative non-alcoholic hamper option offered at checkout
- [ ] "Gift card" option for recipient to schedule delivery date/time
- [ ] "Deliver to recipient over 18" checkbox at ordering

## 6. ZANZIBAR vs. MAINLAND DIFFERENCES

| **Requirement** | **Mainland Tanzania** | **Zanzibar** |
|-----------------|----------------------|--------------|
| **Legal Framework** | Intoxicating Liquors Act (CAP 146) | Separate Zanzibar Liquor Law |
| **License Cost** | TZS 200,000-1,000,000 (annual) | Typically higher, verify locally |
| **Permitted Hours** | 09:00-21:00 sales, 09:00-22:00 delivery | Often stricter, shorter hours |
| **Dry Zones** | LGA-specific | More extensive dry zones |
| **Enforcement** | Local government authorities | Zanzibar Commission for Religion & Culture |
| **Reciprocity** | Mainland license not valid in Zanzibar | Separate Zanzibar license required |

**Recommendation**: If planning nationwide operations, obtain separate licenses for Mainland and Zanzibar. Zanzibar has stricter enforcement in some areas.

## 7. CORPORATE GIFTING SPECIAL PROVISIONS

### **Company Liquor Gifting**
| **Provision** | **Requirement** |
|---------------|-----------------|
| **Bulk Purchase Permit** | Required for orders > TZS 500,000 value |
| **Recipient Verification** | Still mandatory even for corporate orders |
| **Employee Gifting** | Company can gift employees with proper documentation |
| **Client/Partner Gifting** | Requires recipient age verification each time |
| **Documentation** | Must maintain records of all corporate gifts for 3 years |

### **Recommended Corporate Process**
1. **Pre-verify** all recipient ages before corporate order
2. **Include gift note** stating "Recipient confirms over 18 years"
3. **Delivery to office** rather than residential (easier verification)
4. **Schedule delivery** during business hours (09:00-17:00)
5. **Designate company contact** to receive and verify delivery

## 8. DATA PROTECTION & PRIVACY

### **Required Practices**
- **Order information** stored securely per Tanzania data protection guidelines
- **ID photocopies** kept only as long as necessary for verification (min 2 years max)
- **Customer consent** obtained for WhatsApp/SMS communications
- **Delivery photos** stored securely, not shared without consent
- **Financial data** compliant with TRA requirements

### **Prohibited Practices**
- **Sharing customer data** with third parties without consent
- **Posting delivery photos** on social media without explicit permission
- **Storing IDs indefinitely** beyond compliance requirements

## 11. FINAL RECOMMENDATIONS

### **Launch Strategy**
1. **Start without alcohol** - Launch business with flower/hampers/cakes only
2. **6-month compliance review** - Before adding alcohol hampers
3. **Build relationships** with LGA officials early
4. **Document everything** - From training to deliveries to ID verifications
5. **Monitor competitor licensing** - Learn from those already doing alcohol delivery

### **Risk Mitigation**
1. **Never deliver alcohol** without verified age at point of handover
2. **Never promise delivery** to prohibited zones without LGA confirmation
3. **Never operate without current licenses** - Risk too high
4. **Have non-alcohol alternative** always available at checkout
5. **Build compliance cost** into pricing from day one

### **Cost of Compliance**
- **Initial setup**: TZS 1,000,000 - 3,000,000 (licenses, legal, training)
- **Annual ongoing**: TZS 500,000 - 1,000,000 (renewals, training, audits)
- **As percentage of revenue**: 2-5% depending on alcohol offerings

### **Competitive Advantage Through Compliance**
- Businesses fully compliant can market "officially licensed and regulated"
- Reduces customer skepticism (common in Tanzanian e-commerce)
- Enables corporate gifting opportunities (many companies require compliant vendors)
- Enables expansion to Z untapped markets later
